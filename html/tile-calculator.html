FOLLOW THESE STEPS

  1. Measure the length and width of your chosen tile, then enter the values into the tile calculator.

  2. Identify the cost per tile and input the corresponding amount.

  3. Measure the area you want to cover. You can do this in two ways:

    • Enter the total area (in square meters or square feet).

    • Enter the dimensions (length and width) in units such as centimeters, inches, or feet.
      After measuring, input the values into the calculator.

    • Once all fields are completed, the calculator will automatically display the number of tiles required, including an additional 5 – 10% allowance for cutting, waste, and potential future repairs. It will also calculate the estimated price range to help you determine your overall budget for the purchase.
